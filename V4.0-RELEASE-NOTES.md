# Contract IQ v4.0 Release Notes

**Release Date**: November 18, 2025  
**Code Name**: Customer Revenue Intelligence Transformation  
**Previous Version**: v3.5 (OpenAI Integration Complete)

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Version 4.0 represents a **complete business model transformation** from vendor/procurement contract management to **customer revenue intelligence** for B2B SaaS companies. This pivotrepositions Contract IQ as a revenue operations platform focused on churn prevention, expansion opportunities, and renewal optimization.

**Key Business Model Shift**:
- **From**: B2B vendor contract negotiation support
- **To**: Customer revenue intelligence & retention optimization
- **Target Audience**: RevOps, CS Ops, Sales Ops teams managing 200+ customer MSAs
- **Core Value Prop**: Prevent churn, spot expansion opportunities, maximize customer lifetime value

---
