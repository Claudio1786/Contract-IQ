## 🎯 USAGE GUIDE

### When to Use Each Variation:

**Variation 1 (Warm Network):**
- Former colleagues who trust you
- LinkedIn connections you've interacted with before
- Friends/family in relevant industries
- People who've asked "how's the startup going?"

**Variation 2 (Cold Outreach):**
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator prospects
- Companies you researched but have no connection to
- Decision-makers you found via company websites
- Cold LinkedIn DMs or InMail

**Variation 3 (Existing Customer/Referral):**
- Someone specifically referred you to this person
- Warm intro from a mutual contact
- Follow-up from a conference/networking event
- Investor or advisor introduced you

### Personalization Checklist:

✅ Replace [First Name] with their actual name

## 🚀 QUICK TIPS

1. **Send emails Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM** (highest open rates)
2. **Keep subject lines under 50 characters** (mobile-friendly)
3. **Always include a P.S.** (most-read part of emails)
4. **Follow up after 3 days** if no response (use a shorter version)
5. **Offer multiple time slots** if they don't use Calendly
