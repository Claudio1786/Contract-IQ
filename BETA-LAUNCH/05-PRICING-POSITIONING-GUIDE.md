## 🎯 POSITIONING ANGLES

### Angle #1: Scarcity ("Only 3 Spots Available")

**Script:**
*"I'm only taking 3 beta customers this month because I want to personally handle the onboarding and manual extraction for each one. After these 3 spots are filled, the next customers will pay $2,000/month when we launch publicly in Q2."*

**Why this works:**  
- Creates urgency (fear of missing out)
- Justifies the white-glove service (limited capacity)
- Makes the prospect feel special (exclusive access)

---

### Angle #2: ROI ("Pays for Itself in 1 Missed Renewal")

**Script:**
*"Let me ask: How much revenue do you lose when you miss a single renewal? Most companies say $50K-$100K. Contract IQ costs $12,000/year. So if we help you catch just 1 missed renewal, you've paid for the product 5-10x over."*

**Why this works:**  
- Shifts focus from cost to value
- Makes $1,000/month feel like a no-brainer
- Ties directly to their pain point (missed renewals)

---

### Angle #3: Risk Reversal ("Try It for 3 Months, Cancel If It Doesn't Work")

**Script:**
*"Beta customers commit to 3 months minimum. After that, you can cancel anytime. So worst case, you're out $3,000. Best case, you save $50K+ in Year 1 from recovered renewals. Risk vs. reward is pretty skewed in your favor."*

**Why this works:**  
- Lowers perceived risk
- Makes the ask feel smaller ($3K vs $12K/year)
- Confidence signal (we believe it'll work)

---

### Angle #4: Comparison ("50% Cheaper Than Competitors")

**Script:**
*"Most contract management tools (like Ironclad or ContractWorks) cost $2,000-$5,000/month and require months of implementation. Contract IQ is $1,000/month during beta, we integrate with Salesforce in 1 week, and I personally handle the setup. You're getting enterprise-level features at startup pricing."*

**Why this works:**  
- Anchors against higher-priced competitors
- Highlights speed (1 week vs months)
- Reinforces value (enterprise features + personal service)

---

### Angle #5: Social Proof ("2 Slots Already Filled")

**Script:**
*"I've already filled 2 of the 3 beta slots this week. The last spot is yours if you want it, but I need to know by Friday so I can move forward with onboarding."*

**Why this works:**  
- Creates urgency (last chance)
- Social proof (other companies already signed)
- Forces a decision (deadline: Friday)

## 🧠 PRICING PSYCHOLOGY TACTICS

### Tactic #1: Anchor High, Then Discount

**Bad:** *"The beta costs $1,000/month."*

**Good:** *"The public launch price will be $2,000/month, but beta customers get 50% off — so $1,000/month. That's locked in for 12 months."*

**Why:** The $2,000 anchor makes $1,000 feel like a steal.

---

### Tactic #2: Annual vs Monthly Framing

**Bad:** *"It's $12,000/year."*

**Good:** *"It's $1,000/month — less than the cost of one missed renewal."*

**Why:** $1,000/month feels smaller than $12,000/year (even though it's the same).

---

### Tactic #3: Break Down Cost Per Contract

**Bad:** *"It's $1,000/month."*

**Good:** *"If you have 20 contracts, that's $50/contract/month to never miss a renewal. Compare that to losing $100K on a single missed renewal."*

**Why:** Makes the price feel trivial compared to the risk.

---

### Tactic #4: "Not Ready to Spend?" → Reframe as Investment

**Bad:** *"I understand. Let me know when you're ready."*

**Good:** *"I hear you. Let me reframe it: You're not spending $1,000/month. You're investing $1,000/month to protect $500K-$2M in annual recurring revenue. That's a 50:1 ROI if we catch just 1 missed renewal."*

**Why:** Shifts mindset from expense to investment.

---

### Tactic #5: Payment Terms (Make It Easy to Say Yes)

**Bad:** *"The beta costs $12,000/year upfront."*

**Good:** *"Beta customers pay monthly ($1,000/month). No upfront commitment. Cancel after 3 months if it's not working."*

**Why:** Lowers barrier to entry (monthly billing, no long-term lock-in).

## 🚀 PRICING OBJECTION RESPONSES

### Objection: "That's too expensive."

**Response #1 (ROI):**  
*"I hear you. Let me ask: How much revenue do you lose when you miss a single renewal? If the answer is more than $12K/year, then Contract IQ pays for itself in 1 missed renewal."*

**Response #2 (Comparison):**  
*"Compared to what? Most contract management tools cost $2,000-$5,000/month. Contract IQ is $1,000/month during beta — literally 50-80% cheaper than competitors."*

**Response #3 (Break It Down):**  
*"If you have 20 contracts, that's $50/contract/month. Compare that to the $100K you'd lose from a single missed renewal. It's not expensive — it's cheap insurance."*

---

### Objection: "Can you do $500/month instead?"

**Response #1 (Hold the Line):**  
*"I wish I could, but $1,000/month is already 50% off the public launch price ($2,000/month). Plus, I'm personally handling the manual extraction for the first month, which costs me $2,000 in labor. I'm literally breaking even at $1,000/month."*

**Response #2 (Offer a Bonus Instead):**  
*"I can't go lower on price, but here's what I can do: If you commit today, I'll throw in an extra month free. So you get 4 months for the price of 3 ($3,000 instead of $4,000)."*

**Response #3 (Annual Discount):**  
*"If you pay annually upfront ($12,000), I can give you 2 months free. So you'd pay $10,000/year instead of $12,000/year. That's effectively $833/month."*

---

### Objection: "We need approval from our CFO."

**Response:**  
*"Totally understand. Here's a one-pager you can send to your CFO with the ROI breakdown:*

- *Cost: $12,000/year*
- *Value: Save $50K-$200K/year from recovered renewals*
- *ROI: 5-10x return if we catch just 1 missed renewal*
- *Risk: 3-month minimum, cancel anytime after*

*Happy to hop on a 15-minute call with you + your CFO to walk through the numbers. Does that help?"*

## 🎯 FINAL REMINDER: POSITIONING = CONFIDENCE

**The #1 rule of pricing:** If you sound uncertain about the price, prospects will negotiate.

**Bad:** *"It's $1,000/month... but I can probably go lower if that's too much?"*

**Good:** *"Beta customers pay $1,000/month — 50% off the public launch price. That's locked in for 12 months. Want to secure your spot?"*

**Confidence signals:**
- Don't apologize for the price
- Don't offer discounts unless they ask
- Always frame as an investment, not a cost
- Use anchors ($2,000/month public pricing)
- Create urgency (only 3 slots, deadline Friday)
