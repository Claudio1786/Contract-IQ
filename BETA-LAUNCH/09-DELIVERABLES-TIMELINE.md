# Beta Customer Deliverables Timeline (Months 1-3)
**Created:** November 18, 2025  
**Purpose:** Show beta customers exactly what they'll get and when  
**Use Case:** Share after signing agreement to set expectations

---

## 📅 MONTH 1: Onboarding + Manual Extraction

### Week 1: Kickoff
**What happens:**
- [ ] **Onboarding call** (30 minutes) — Walk through platform, upload flow, dashboard
- [ ] **You send us 10-20 contracts** (PDFs, Word docs, or Google Docs links)
- [ ] **Grant Salesforce OAuth access** (we'll guide you through setup)

**What you get:**
✅ Platform access (login credentials)  
✅ Salesforce integration set up  
✅ Confirmation that we received your contracts  

**Time investment from you:** 45 minutes (30-min call + 15 mins to send contracts)

---

### Week 2: Manual Extraction
**What happens:**
- [ ] **We manually extract key dates** from all your contracts:
  - Renewal dates
  - Auto-renewal terms
  - Notice periods (30/60/90 days)
  - Annual Contract Value (ACV)
  - Total Contract Value (TCV)
- [ ] **We sync all data to Salesforce** (Contract__c custom object)

**What you get:**
✅ All contracts visible in Contract IQ dashboard  
✅ All contracts synced to Salesforce  
✅ Email notification when extraction is complete  

**Time investment from you:** 0 minutes (we do all the work)

---

### Week 3: Review + Feedback
**What happens:**
- [ ] **You review extracted data** in dashboard
- [ ] **15-minute feedback call** — Tell us what's accurate, what needs fixing
- [ ] **We fix any errors** within 24 hours

**What you get:**
✅ Corrected data (if needed)  
✅ Confidence that all renewal dates are accurate  

**Time investment from you:** 30 minutes (15-min review + 15-min call)

---

### Week 4: Automated Alerts Go Live
**What happens:**
- [ ] **We activate automated alerts** (90/60/30 days before renewals)
- [ ] **We test alerts** with upcoming renewals
- [ ] **15-minute feedback call** — Confirm alerts are working correctly

**What you get:**
✅ Automated email alerts for upcoming renewals  
✅ Alerts sent to Account owners in Salesforce  
✅ Copy of every alert sent to you (for transparency)  

**Time investment from you:** 15 minutes (feedback call)

---

## 📞 SUPPORT THROUGHOUT BETA

**How to reach us:**
- **Urgent issues (alert not sent, Salesforce sync broken):** Slack DM or email (< 4-hour response)
- **Non-urgent questions (feature requests, minor bugs):** Email (< 24-hour response)
- **General feedback:** Bi-weekly calls (scheduled in advance)

**What counts as urgent?**
- Automated alert didn't send
- Salesforce sync stopped working
- Dashboard won't load
- Contract data is incorrect and needs immediate fix

**What's non-urgent?**
- Feature requests
- Minor UI bugs
- Questions about how to use a feature
