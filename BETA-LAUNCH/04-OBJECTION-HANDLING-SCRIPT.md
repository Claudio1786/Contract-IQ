# Objection Handling Script
**Created:** November 18, 2025  
**Purpose:** Handle common objections during beta sales calls  
**Goal:** Convert objections into closed deals

---

## 🧠 MINDSET: OBJECTIONS = BUYING SIGNALS

**Key principle:** If someone objects, they're engaged. They're just looking for reassurance.

Your job: **Acknowledge → Reframe → Ask for the close.**

---

## 🛑 COMMON OBJECTIONS & RESPONSES

---

### OBJECTION #1: "We already track renewals manually in Salesforce."

**Translation:** *"Why do we need this?"*

**Response:**
"Totally understand — most teams I talk to do that too. Quick question: **How often do renewals slip through the cracks?** Even just 1-2 per year?

From what I've seen, manual tracking works until it doesn't. One AE forgets to log a renewal date, or someone leaves the company and their contracts aren't documented.

Contract IQ is like insurance. You might catch 90% of renewals manually, but **that 10% you miss costs you $50K-$100K in lost upsell opportunities.**

Beta customers are getting 50% off specifically because we want to prove we can save you that 10%. Want to test it out?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"If we can prove we catch at least 1-2 renewals you would've missed this year, is $1,000/month worth it?"*

---

### OBJECTION #2: "I don't have time to onboard a new tool right now."

**Translation:** *"Sounds like work."*

**Response:**
"I totally get it — the last thing you need is another project. That's actually **why we're doing a beta with white-glove service.**

Here's how it works:
1. You send me 10-20 contracts (takes 10 minutes)
2. **I** manually extract all the key dates and sync them to Salesforce
3. You review the data (15 minutes) and approve it
4. We set up automated alerts

**Total time investment from you: ~30 minutes in Week 1, then 15 minutes every 2 weeks for feedback.**

The alternative is spending 2-3 hours per quarter manually tracking renewals, right? This actually **saves** you time.

Want to try it?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"If I handle 90% of the setup work, can you commit 30 minutes in Week 1?"*

---

### OBJECTION #3: "What if the AI makes mistakes extracting contract data?"

**Translation:** *"I don't trust automation with critical data."*

**Response:**
"Great question — and honestly, **that's exactly why we're starting with manual extraction during the beta.**

I'm not trusting AI with your contracts yet. **I'll personally review every contract and extract the data manually.** You'll approve everything before it syncs to Salesforce.

Once we're confident the data is 100% accurate, we'll transition to AI-assisted extraction (which you'll still review).

So during beta, you're getting human accuracy + manual review. Zero risk of mistakes.

Does that make sense?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"If I personally guarantee the accuracy of every extraction, are you comfortable moving forward?"*

---

### OBJECTION #4: "We're not ready to spend $1,000/month right now."

**Translation:** *"That's too expensive."*

**Response:**
"I hear you — $1,000/month is a real investment. Let me ask: **How much revenue do you lose when you miss a renewal?**

Most companies I talk to say 1 missed renewal costs them $50K-$100K in lost upsell opportunities.

**ROI math:**
- Contract IQ costs $12,000/year
- If we help you catch **just 1 missed renewal**, you've paid for the product 5-10x over

Plus, beta customers get 50% off ($1,000 vs $2,000), so you're saving $12,000 in Year 1 alone.

**And here's the kicker:** You can cancel after 3 months if it's not working. So worst case, you're out $3,000. Best case, you save $50K+ in Year 1.

Does that math work for you?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"If we catch even 1 missed renewal in the next 3 months, are you comfortable with the investment?"*

---

### OBJECTION #5: "Can we wait until after Q1? We're too busy right now."

**Translation:** *"I'll think about it later."*

**Response:**
"Totally understand — Q4/Q1 is always crazy. Here's the thing though:

**The beta has only 3 spots, and they're first-come, first-served.** If you wait until Q2, the beta will be full and you'll pay $2,000/month instead of $1,000/month.

**Plus, if you start now, we'll handle all the heavy lifting during your busy season.** You just send us contracts and we'll extract everything manually. By the time Q2 hits, you'll already have Contract IQ running and saving you time.

So it's actually the **least disruptive time to start** because we're doing the work, not you.

Want to lock in your beta spot now and onboard in January?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"Can we at least reserve your beta spot now, and we'll start the onboarding whenever you're ready?"*

---

### OBJECTION #6: "We use HubSpot, not Salesforce."

**Translation:** *"This won't work for us."*

**Response:**
"Ah, good to know! Right now Contract IQ is Salesforce-only, but **we're planning to add HubSpot support based on beta feedback.**

Here's what I'd propose:

**Option 1:** If HubSpot integration is a dealbreaker, I can add you to our waitlist for when we launch HubSpot support (likely Q2 2026).

**Option 2:** If you're willing to be our **first HubSpot beta customer**, I'll work with our dev team to prioritize HubSpot integration and give you an extra month free ($1,000 credit).

**Would either of those work?**"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"If we prioritize HubSpot integration for you, would you be willing to be our first HubSpot beta customer?"*

---

### OBJECTION #7: "I need to run this by my team first."

**Translation:** *"I'm not the decision-maker."*

**Response:**
"Totally makes sense — this is a team decision. Quick question: **Who else needs to be involved?** (Head of Sales? RevOps? CFO?)

Here's what I'd suggest:
1. I'll send you the beta program one-pager (1-page summary you can forward)
2. You share it with your team
3. I can hop on a quick 15-minute call with everyone to answer questions

**Timeline-wise, we're down to 3 beta spots, so I'd recommend looping your team in this week if you want to secure the 50% discount.**

Does that work?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"If your team approves, can we lock in your beta spot by Friday?"*

---

### OBJECTION #8: "Can you show me the product first? I want to see a demo."

**Translation:** *"I need to see it before I buy."*

**Response:**
"Absolutely — but here's the thing: **We're still building the beta version, so there's no live demo yet.**

What I can show you is:
1. Sample Salesforce fields (what the extracted data looks like)
2. Screenshots of the manual extraction workflow
3. A walkthrough of the alert system

But honestly, **the beta is the demo.** You'll be one of the first 3 customers testing the product, which is why we're offering 50% off and white-glove service.

**Here's the deal:**
- We onboard you in Week 1
- You test the product for 3 months
- If it's not working, you can cancel (no long-term contract)

So you're not committing to 12 months sight-unseen. You're committing to a 3-month trial at 50% off.

Does that work?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"If you're not happy after Month 1, we'll refund you and part ways. Does that give you enough confidence to move forward?"*

### OBJECTION #9: "We're already using [Competitor Product] for contract management."

**Translation:** *"Why switch?"*

**Response:**
"Oh interesting! How's [Competitor] working for you?

[Listen to their answer]

**What I'm hearing is:** [Restate their pain point]. That's actually exactly what Contract IQ solves differently.

Here's what makes us unique:
- **We're Salesforce-native** (most competitors are standalone tools)
- **We focus on renewal dates + alerts** (not full contract management)
- **We're simpler** (you don't need to train your whole team on a new system)

**Beta offer:** Since you're already using [Competitor], I'll give you a **side-by-side trial**. Use Contract IQ alongside [Competitor] for 3 months and see which one your team prefers.

If Contract IQ wins, you switch. If [Competitor] is better, you cancel. **Risk-free.**

Sound fair?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"Can we run a 3-month side-by-side test and see which tool your team prefers?"*

### OBJECTION #10: "I'm interested, but I need to see a case study first."

**Translation:** *"Prove this works."*

You'd be one of the first 3 customers, which means:

2. You shape the product based on your feedback
3. **You become the case study** (which we'll pay you $1,000 to do)

So instead of waiting for someone else to prove it works, **you get paid to help us prove it works.**

**Here's the deal:**
- Test Contract IQ for 3 months
- If it works, we'll feature you in a case study (you get a $1,000 credit)
- If it doesn't work, cancel after Month 3 (no penalty)

You literally can't lose.

Want in?"

**Ask for the close:**  
*"If we pay you $1,000 to be a case study customer, are you in?"*

## 🎯 OBJECTION HANDLING FRAMEWORK

**Step 1: Acknowledge**  
*"I totally understand that concern…"*  
*"Great question — here's how we handle that…"*  
*"That makes sense. Let me explain…"*

**Step 2: Reframe**  
*"What most customers don't realize is…"*  
*"Here's what's different about Contract IQ…"*  
*"Let me show you how this actually saves you time/money…"*

**Step 3: Ask for the close**  
*"If I can guarantee [X], are you comfortable moving forward?"*  
*"Does that address your concern? Should we lock in your beta spot?"*  
*"What would it take for you to say yes today?"*

## 🚀 ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

### Technique #1: The "Feel, Felt, Found" Method

**Template:**
*"I totally understand how you **feel**. Other beta customers **felt** the same way before they joined. Here's what they **found**: [Insert positive outcome]."*

**Example:**
*"I totally understand how you feel about the $1,000/month investment. Other customers felt the same way until they realized they were losing $50K/year from missed renewals. What they found was that Contract IQ paid for itself within the first month."*

---

### Technique #2: The "Worst Case" Scenario

**Template:**
*"What's the worst that happens? You try it for 3 months, it doesn't work, and you cancel. Best case? You save $50K-$100K in Year 1. Risk vs. reward is pretty skewed in your favor, right?"*

---

### Technique #3: The "Social Proof" Close

**Template:**
*"I've already signed 2 beta customers this week (XYZ Company and ABC Corp). You'd be in good company. Want to join them?"*

---

### Technique #4: The "Scarcity" Close

**Template:**
*"I only have 1 beta spot left. If you're interested, I need to know by Friday so I can lock it in. After that, you'll have to wait until Q2 and pay $2,000/month instead of $1,000/month."*

## 📞 CALL SCRIPT: OBJECTION → CLOSE

**Prospect:** *"I'm interested, but I need to think about it."*

**You:**  
"Totally fair — this is a real investment. Can I ask: **What specifically do you need to think about?** Is it the pricing, the time commitment, or something else?"

[Listen to their answer]

**You:**  
"Got it. [Address their specific objection using one of the responses above.]

Here's what I'd suggest: Let's lock in your beta spot now (so you don't lose the 50% discount), and we'll schedule the onboarding call for next week. That gives you time to think, but ensures you don't miss out on the beta pricing.

Sound good?"

**Prospect:** *"Okay, let's do it."*

**You:**  
"Awesome! I'll send you the beta agreement right now. Sign it today and we'll get you onboarded next week. Sound good?"

## ✅ OBJECTION HANDLING CHECKLIST

**Before the call:**
- [ ] Review the 10 most common objections above
- [ ] Prepare 2-3 "social proof" examples (even if they're hypothetical)
- [ ] Have the beta agreement ready to send immediately after close

**During the call:**
- [ ] Listen for the **real** objection (not the surface-level one)
- [ ] Acknowledge their concern first (don't jump straight to rebuttal)
- [ ] Reframe the objection as a benefit (e.g., "That's exactly why we're doing beta")
- [ ] Always ask for the close after handling the objection

**After the call:**
- [ ] Send a follow-up email summarizing how you addressed their objection
- [ ] Include the beta agreement or next steps
- [ ] Set a deadline ("Let me know by Friday so I can reserve your spot")
