# 🚀 Contract IQ Beta Program
## Never Miss a Renewal Again

---

### 💡 THE PROBLEM

Revenue teams lose **$50K-$200K annually** because contract renewals fall through the cracks.

**Why this happens:**
- Contracts scattered across email, Google Drive, Salesforce attachments
- AEs manually log renewal dates (or forget entirely)
- No automated alerts before renewals
- RevOps spends hours building spreadsheets to track everything

**The result?** You miss upsell opportunities because you didn't reach out before the renewal deadline.

---

### ✅ THE SOLUTION: Contract IQ

**Contract IQ** plugs into Salesforce and automatically:

1. **Extracts key dates** from customer contracts  
   *(Renewal dates, auto-renewal terms, notice periods)*

2. **Syncs to Salesforce**  
   *(Custom fields populated automatically)*

3. **Triggers alerts** 90/60/30 days before renewals  
   *(Automated email/Slack notifications to AEs)*
