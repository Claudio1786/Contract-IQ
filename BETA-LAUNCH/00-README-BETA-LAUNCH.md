## 🚨 COMMON PITFALLS (Avoid These!)

### Pitfall #1: Trying to close beta customers without proof
**Solution:** Use **03-BETA-PROGRAM-ONE-PAGER.md** to show value. If they need more proof, offer case study bonus ($1,000 credit).

### Pitfall #2: Over-promising features that aren't in MVP
**Solution:** Stick to the 5 must-have features in **06-MANUAL-MVP-SPEC.md**. Everything else is "coming in Q2 2026."

### Pitfall #3: Undercharging ("Can you do $500/month?")
**Solution:** Hold the line at $1,000/month. Use **05-PRICING-POSITIONING-GUIDE.md** to reframe objections.

### Pitfall #4: Factory.ai builds wrong features
**Solution:** Weekly check-ins (Monday 10 AM, Friday 4 PM) to demo progress. Refer to **07-WEEK-BY-WEEK-BUILD-SCHEDULE.md** to stay on track.

### Pitfall #5: Beta customer churns after Month 1
**Solution:** Use **09-DELIVERABLES-TIMELINE.md** to set expectations. Show ROI data (renewals caught, revenue recovered) in Month 2-3.
